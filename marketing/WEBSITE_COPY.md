## Hero

**Set the points. Light the route.**

A cozy 3D train puzzle about thoughtful routes and perfectly ordered freight. Guide a little locomotive through 48 authored yards, collect every wagon in its pictured order and bring the whole train home.

**Primary call to action:** Play Switchyard Lanterns

## Three selling cards

### Set the points, light the route

Read the yard, tap the turnouts and preview your plan. Untimed planning, Pause, Step and free Undo let you find a good line at your own pace.

### Put every wagon in its place

Freight is more than a destination: its order matters. Work through 48 yards across six districts, then return for three-star mastery and an unassisted record worth keeping.

### A little railway worth coming home to

Amber station lamps, wooded valleys and original district music make room for a quiet daily ritual. Take a Daily Dispatch, complete freight goals and grow your dispatcher record.

## A small piece of railway lore

The valley does not sleep all at once. Orchard crates leave before the last market closes; quarry wagons wait beyond the bend; a station lamp stays lit for the final arrival. You are the dispatcher who joins those little journeys together, one set of points at a time.

## Inspiration

The pleasure of a model railway meets the satisfaction of a clear logic puzzle: a visible route, a concrete freight order and the moment a carefully planned train rolls into the station. The controls focus on routing decisions rather than fast steering.

## Technical and service copy

Made in Godot 4.5.1 with the Compatibility renderer. Portrait Android is the primary platform, with a Web export prepared as an additional way to play. Progress is saved locally. The Android integration includes Google Play Games and AdMob, configured with test advertising IDs and disabled production Play Games mappings in this first pass. Online records, production ads and final backend/signing setup require the owner's configuration and testing. The Web version keeps local play available without pretending that native-only services or earned rewards succeeded.

Do not turn source/export support into a claim of physical-device, browser-interaction or audible-output validation; see the current handoff for actual evidence and limits. No Windows distribution is planned.
