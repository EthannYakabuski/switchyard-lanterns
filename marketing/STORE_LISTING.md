## Long description

A little railway. A thoughtful route. A light waiting at the end.

Take the dispatcher's seat in Switchyard Lanterns, a 3D train puzzle about putting every wagon in its proper place. Read the freight manifest, set the points and watch your locomotive follow the route you planned. Collect the right cargo in the right order, then bring the whole train safely back to the station.

PLAN A LINE WORTH FOLLOWING
Tap clear A/B turnout controls, preview the route and send your train on its way. Pause to think, advance a step at a time or undo a decision and try another line. Planning is untimed, and ordinary retries are free. Cargo names and shapes help you read the order alongside its colors.

48 YARDS, ONE LIVING VALLEY
Start with your first lantern at Pine Junction. Carry orchard freight, work the canal sidings, wind through the quarry and keep the midnight railway moving before taking on Grand Dispatch. Six districts each bring eight authored yards, with changing routes, manifests and freight constraints.

MAKE A GOOD ROUTE A GREAT ONE
Finish a delivery to move forward, then return for three-star mastery. Earn dispatcher experience, unlock cosmetic liveries and build a record of reliable work. Optional earned hints offer a hand; assisted runs stay separate from unassisted mastery records.

A REASON TO RETURN
Take on the Daily Dispatch, complete freight goals and collect a seven-day welcome calendar. Follow 20 achievements and three record boards as your little railway grows into a familiar place.

A RAILWAY WITH A LITTLE WARMTH
Amber lamps, wooded valleys, wagons and station details frame every yard. Original district music and small mechanical sounds give each departure its own rhythm. Portrait touch controls and local progress make it easy to return to your next unfinished plan.

Set the points. Light the route. Bring every wagon home.
