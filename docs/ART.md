# Illustrated artwork

The app icon and feature graphic are original promotional raster illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-22. They are marketing art, not screenshots of gameplay. The game's actual scenery uses the separately credited Kenney models; illustration detail and lighting exceed the low-poly gameplay rendering.

The selected originals were technically resampled full-frame to the store sizes without cropping, compositing or semantic retouching. Original icon: 1254x1254; original feature: 1795x876. Final PNGs are RGB 512x512 and 1024x500. Both were visually inspected at full export size, the icon at 64x64 and the feature at 512x250. Title spelling, focal readability and distinct game mood were checked. Device/audio/gameplay QA is separate.

## App icon prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / illustrated mobile game app icon.
Asset: one finished raster app icon for "Switchyard Lanterns", 512 by 512 pixels, square opaque full bleed.
Create premium stylized 3D illustrated game key art: a charming chunky blue-teal diesel locomotive seen close from a low three-quarter front angle, with a large warm amber headlamp and one brass railway lantern mounted beside the cab. The locomotive's friendly strong silhouette and lamp are the unmistakable central subject, filling about 75 percent of the frame. It rounds a short curving track through a lush miniature rail yard at indigo dusk. Rich blue painted metal, copper/brass trim, rounded toy-like low-poly proportions, carefully shaded wheels, real timber sleepers, subtle moss and a few flowering grasses. Behind it are a tiny warmly lit depot window, one red freight wagon and soft pine silhouettes, reduced in contrast so the engine stays readable at 48 pixels. A warm lantern glow cuts across cool violet-blue evening haze; convincing rim light, material texture and depth, confident saturated colors. Composition is bold and uncluttered, all essential parts well inside a 10 percent safe edge for rounded Android masks. This should feel like polished bespoke game illustration, not a flat vector symbol, generic train emoji, plastic product photo or screenshot. No text, letters, numerals, logos, badge border, watermark, extra vehicles overlapping the focal subject, photorealism, pixel art or UI.

## Feature graphic prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / illustrated store feature graphic.
Asset: one finished wide raster feature image for the mobile puzzle game "Switchyard Lanterns", 1024 by 500 pixels, landscape, opaque full bleed.
Premium stylized 3D illustrated game key art with the detail and composition of a professionally painted mobile-game poster. At indigo dusk a charming blue-teal toy-proportioned diesel locomotive enters from the right foreground on curving timber-sleeper railway tracks, warm amber headlamp and brass lantern illuminating the rails. A red boxcar, a small golden freight wagon and a green tanker create a clear ordered train behind it. The middle distance reveals an inviting miniature switching yard with visible branch tracks, a timber-and-brick depot, freight crates, a little bridge over a reflective stream and warm semaphore lanterns. Beyond are layered pine hills and a violet dusk sky. Specific rendered materials, colorful freight, tiny scenic details and depth make the environment rewarding to inspect, while a strong diagonal of glowing rails leads the eye from the engine toward the title area. Calm puzzle-planning mood, purposeful railway charm, no collisions or stunts. Rich cool indigo and teal with amber/coral highlights; warm windows and volumetric evening haze. Preserve quiet dark sky in the upper-left quarter. Integrate only the exact title "SWITCHYARD LANTERNS" there as two beautifully crafted readable cream-and-brass lines of compact railway-poster lettering with a subtle dark shadow; title remains legible when reduced. No other text. Keep all important title/engine detail within a 6 percent safe edge, compose natively for 1024:500. Rounded low-poly model proportions with rich illustrated lighting, not pixel art, flat geometry or a screenshot. No UI, phone frame, ratings, laurels, badges, watermarks or borrowed logos.
